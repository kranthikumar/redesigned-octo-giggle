# Enjoy Worlds

A scroll-driven use-cases page for Enjoy. As you scroll, a field of particles re-forms into a different "world" for each persona and the background shifts to that world's palette:

| World | Who | Particle scene |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | — | orbiting sun |
| The Workshop | Founders & builders | stacked isometric blueprint layers |
| The Map Room | Product managers | topographic contour lines |
| The Front Desk | Small businesses | a wall of forms and schedule cards |
| The Switchboard | Knowledge workers | apps orbiting a hub, messages flowing inward |
| The Atlas | Everyone else (8 more personas) | a loop of constellations |
| Your world | — | a portal |

It's a single self-contained `index.html` (vanilla JS + canvas 2D, no build step). Open it in a browser.

The particles scatter away from the pointer, and the page respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
